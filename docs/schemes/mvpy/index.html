Mukhyamantri Vidyarthi Pratibha Yojna eligibility, benefits, how to apply

मुख्यमंत्री विद्यार्थी प्रतिभा योजना ముఖ్యమంత్రి విద్యార్థి ప్రతిభా యోజన

This scheme is to provide scholarships to the students studying in classes 9th to 12th for enabling them to bear the expenses required for their studies.

Benefits

Benefit amount not stated on the official page

Amount of Assistance:

1. IX to X Class : ₹ 5,000/- Per annum

1. XI to XII Class :₹ 10,000/- Per annum.

Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of GNCT of Delhi.

1. There is a Minimum of 50% marks required for IX to X and 60% marks required for XI to XII Classes.

1. Annual Income Limit – Criteria for annual family income ₹ 8 Lacs at par annum with the EWS income limit, subject to exclusions notified by the Government of India. If the student scores 75% and above marks in the previous class, no income limit is applicable for SC/ST/OBC category.

1. The applicant should upload a Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC) issued in the name of the student or his father’s name issued by the SDM/Deputy Commissioner (Revenue), GNCTD. However, the Domicile Certificate of Delhi from the Revenue Deptt. Will be entertained for the SC beneficiary only, if the caste certificate is issued by the outside state authority instead of the competent authority of Govt. of NCT of Delhi.

1. Repeaters in a particular class will not be eligible for this scheme.

1. Bank Account should be in the name of the student (a Joint Bank Account with parent/Guardian is also permissible), active and seeded with the Aadhaar no of the student.

Documents

  • For SC/ST/OBC students, a Caste certificate is issued by the concerned office of the District Magistrate of Revenue Department, GNCT of Delhi.
  • Annual family income certificate issued by the Revenue Department, GNCT of Delhi.
  • Proof of studying in classes 9th to 12th in the schools in Delhi.
  • Mark sheet of the previous year.

How to apply

- Applicants have to visit https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/

1. Under Citizen Corner click on New User

1. Click on select the document – Aadhar or Voter ID

1. Enter your Aadhar Card Number/Voter ID Card Number

1. Type the Captcha in the box shown

- Citizen Registration Form will Open for registration

1. Fill up all fields including details of the Present Residential Address

1. Enter Captcha, and Click on Continue.

1. Login ID and Password will be received on the given mobile number/e-mail address.

1. Now Registration on the e-district portal is complete

- Registration for Financial Assistance Services

1. Click on Registered User Login

1. Enter the given login ID and Password

1. Enter Captcha and Click on Login

1. Go to Main Page (Main Page) will open click on apply online.

1. Drop Box apply for services will open – click

1. A list of Departments providing online services shall open –

1. Select Department of Social Welfare.

1. Click on the scheme name to Apply.

1. Check the BASIC/PERSONAL DETAILS FORM and click on Continue.

1. The form will open fill up all required fields & upload all required documents as per the requirement of the scheme selected.

1. Then click next and upload a photograph

1. Then click on finish.

1. One OTP will be received on the registered mobile number.

1. Enter OTP and Submit.

1. Acknowledgement of successful submission shall be generated.
