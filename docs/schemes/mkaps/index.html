Mukhyamantri Kanya Abhibhawak Pension Scheme eligibility, benefits, how to apply

मुख्यमंत्री कन्या अभियोजक पेंशन योजना ముఖ్యమంత్రి కన్యా అభిభవక్ పింఛను పథకం

The Government of Madhya Pradesh has launched a scheme entitled “Mukhyamantri Kanya Abhibhavak Pension Yojana” on 01 April 201, for the welfare of citizens having one daughter.

Benefits

Rs. 600 / - per month to the couple.

Benefits

Rs. 600 / - per month to the couple.

Eligibility

Eligibility

1. The native place should be Madhya Pradesh

1. The benefits under this scheme can be availed to the couple having an only daughter as a child.

1. The age of either of the beneficiary couples is 60 years or more.

1. The beneficiary couple should not be income-tax payers.

1. The children of the parent(beneficiary couple) should be daughters and should be married.

Documents

  • List of required documents
  • Certificate in respect of couple having an only girl child and no living son.
  • Self-declaration certificate regarding not income taxpayer.
  • Certificate regarding age and residence.
  • Joint photo of the couple / one photo in case of singles.
  • Certificate of death of husband issued by the competent authority for the widow and abandoned women / attested copy of the order issued by the Honorable Court for abandonment.

How to apply

Application process

Apply with the prescribed application form in the Gram Panchayat / District Panchayat in the rural area of your original residence,

And In the office of the Municipal Corporation / Municipality / Municipal Council in the urban area.

Application Process

Visit the official site.

On the home page click on "Social Pension Pension and Financial Assistance Schemes / सामाजिक पेंशन पेंशन एवं आर्थिक सहायता योजनाएं".

On the next page click on "Online Application for Pension Schemes / पेंशन योजनाओं हेतु ऑनलाइन आवेदन".

Now provide the required details and submit.

Now application form will be available.

Fill out the application form and provide the required details.
