Widow Pension - Uttar Pradesh: eligibility

विधवा पेंशन-उत्तर प्रदेश వితంతువు పింఛను-ఉత్తరప్రదేశ్

Widow pension in Uttar Pradesh is a social welfare scheme by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to provide financial assistance to widows residing in the state. The scheme is administered by the Women Welfare Department of the state government.

Benefits

Financial Assistance : ₹1,000/- per month

Financial Assistance : ₹1,000/- per month

Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh state.

1. The applicant's husband has died.

1. The age of the applicant should not be less than 18 years.

1. The annual income of the applicant and her family from all sources should not exceed ₹ 2,00,000/-

Documents

  • Colored passport size photo
  • Age related certificate (educational records/provided by medical officer).
  • Income Certificate (provided by Tehsil).
  • Death certificate of husband (local body/gram panchayat).
  • Aadhaar Card/Aadhaar Registration Number (No need to submit separate certificate for age and residence).
  • Photocopy of bank account with IFS code.
  • Mobile number.
  • Not receiving pension from any other scheme of the state or central government related affidavit

How to apply

1. Applicant must visit the official website.1. Click on "Apply Online" .

1. Provide the below required details:

- Personal Details.

- Bank detail.

- Income Details.

1. Upload below document:

- Passport size color photograph.

- Birth/Age certificate.

- Husband Death certificate.

1. Declaration.

1. Click on submit.
