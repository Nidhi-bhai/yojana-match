State/ UT · Assam

Post Graduate Scholarship for Ph.D./LLM Students eligibility, benefits, how to apply

पीएचडी/एल. एल. एम. छात्रों के लिए स्नातकोत्तर छात्रवृत्ति పీహెచ్డీ/ఎల్ఎల్ఎం విద్యార్థులకు పోస్ట్ గ్రాడ్యుయేట్ స్కాలర్షిప్

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students of Assam pursuing PhD and LLM degree courses in recognized institutions. It benefits academically meritorious students who have obtained at least 55% marks in their last qualifying examination and are domiciled in Assam.

Benefits

Benefit amount not stated on the official page

Financial Assistance: Scholarship is provided to the poor students to help them achieve the benefits of Higher Education.

Mode of Disbursement: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

_*The applicant must maintain registration as Doctor of Philosophy/Post Doctorate Research Scholar in recognized universities._

_*Academic performance standards must be maintained as per university requirements._

Eligibility

- The applicant must have registered their names as Doctor of Philosophy/Post Doctorate Research Scholar in any recognized universities.

- The applicant must be domiciled in Assam.

- The applicant must have obtained at least 55% or above marks in the last qualifying examination.

- The applicant must have uniformly good academic records.

Documents

  • Registration Certificate as Doctor of Philosophy/Post Doctorate Research Scholar from Recognized University.
  • Domicile Certificate of Assam.
  • Mark Sheets of Last Qualifying Examination showing at least 55% marks.
  • Academic Record Documents (transcripts/certificates).

How to apply

Step 1: _Advertisement Publication_

Wait for the official advertisement to be published by the Higher Education Department announcing the opening of applications for the scholarship scheme.

Step 2: _Online Application Submission_

Visit the official portal (yet to be notified) and submit the online application form through the designated university portal with all required information and documents.

Step 3: _Application Processing_

The submitted applications will be received online from the universities and processed by the concerned authorities for initial verification and compilation.

Step 4: _Selection Process_

The selection committee will review all eligible applications and select candidates based on the prescribed eligibility criteria and merit evaluation process.

Step 5: _Financial Sanction_

Successful candidates will have their files moved for financial sanction approval from the competent authority before benefit disbursement.

Step 6: _Benefit Disbursement_

The scholarship amount will be disbursed through Direct Benefit Transfer mode directly to the bank accounts of selected beneficiaries.
