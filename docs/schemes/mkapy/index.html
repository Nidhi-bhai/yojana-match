State/ UT · Madhya Pradesh

MKAPY: eligibility, documents, how to apply

Mukhyamantri Kanya Abhibhavak Pension Yojana

मुख्यमंत्री कन्या अनुभवक पेंशन योजना ముఖ్యమంత్రి కన్యా అభిభవక్ పెన్షన్ యోజన

The scheme aims to provide financial support to parents or couples who have only daughters. The scheme provides a recurring monthly pension amount of ₹500/- (Rupees 500) to the eligible recipient or couple. The applicant must be a resident of the state of Madhya Pradesh.

Benefits

A monthly pension amount of ₹500/- (Rupees 500) is provided.

- A monthly pension amount of ₹500/- (Rupees 500) is provided.

- The benefit is provided monthly.

- The funds are deposited into the joint savings account of the applicant couple or the single applicant's account maintained at a nationalized bank or Post Office.

_*The beneficiary must continue to fulfill the established eligibility criteria as defined in the rules so that the benefits are not recalled._

Eligibility

- The applicant must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradeah.

- The applicant couple or the single applicant must have a minimum age of 60 years for at least one person at the time of application.

- The applicant couple or the single applicant must have only daughters.

- The applicant must belong to the Below Poverty Line category.

Documents

  • The applicants are not required to attach or upload any documents when submitting the application form.
  • However, the applicants will need to have certain documents available to reference, as the applicants be asked to enter specific document numbers or identification details in the mandatory fields of the form.
  • It is recommend to keep the following on hand while completing the application:
  • Relevant identification documents.
  • Any certificates or credentials referenced in the form.
  • Other supporting documents that contain reference numbers you may need to input.

How to apply

##### *Registration*

Step 1: Visit the Official Website of "Madhya Pradesh Lok Sewa Guarantee Portal" and click "नागरिक पंजीयन (Citizen Registration)".

Step 2: In the registration page, provide the following mandatory information: Name, Gender, Mobile No., Email ID.

Step 3: Create a new password and confirm the same. Fill the captcha code. Verify your Mobile Number via OTP. Finally, click "Register".

##### *Application*

Step 1: Visit the Official Website of "Madhya Pradesh Lok Sewa Guarantee Portal" and click "लॉग इन (Login)".

Step 2: Login using your registered Email ID and the password created during registration.

Step 3: On the homepage, sort the list of services "By Department". Further, select the Department and the respective scheme that you intend to apply for. On the next page, detailed information about the scheme will be displayed.

Step 4: In the scheme's online application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size (fields marked with * are mandatory), and click "Submit". *Alternatively, you may apply via your nearest Lok Sewa Kendra / MP Online / CSC by making a payment of ₹20/-.*
