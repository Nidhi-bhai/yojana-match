SFGCS: eligibility, documents, how to apply

Sudakshya For Girls Child Scholarship

लड़कियों के लिए सुदाक्ष्य बाल छात्रवृत्ति బాలికలకు సుదాక్ష్య స్కాలర్షిప్

This scholarship provides financial assistance to all deserving students who want to pursue higher studies. Under this scholarship scheme, the students fulfilling the eligibility criteria will receive a maintenance allowance and other benefits.

Benefits

Benefit amount not stated on the official page

1. Reimbursement of Admission and Hostel Fee.

1. Maintenance Allowances will be given of ₹1,500/- per month for Hostellers and ₹500/- for Day Scholars.

1. Reimbursable cost of uniform ₹3,000/-.

1. Maintenance Allowances ₹1,000/- per month for Apprenticeship Training after ITI inside the state and ₹1,500/- per month for outside the state.

1. One Month Maintenance Allowances of ₹5,000/- after completion of 6 months on Jobs outside the State.

> Note 01: Benefits will be directly transferred to the bank account of the student.

> Note 02: Maintenance Allowances shall be released on a half-yearly basis.

> Note 03: ST, SC, OBC, Minority, Children of BOC Workers, PwD, MCP, Merit, and other stipend/scholarship holders will get differential amounts only.

Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Odisha state.

1. The applicant must be a girl child.

1. The applicant must belong to the General/ST/SC/OBC/SEBC/EBC category.

1. The applicant must be pursuing I.T.I. / I.T.C., Polytechnic/ Diploma courses.

Documents

  • Aadhaar Card of the student
  • Income Certificate of the Parent/ guardian
  • Caste Certificate of the Student
  • Copy of passbook of Bank account with the account number and IFSC code of the bank branch
  • Passport-size Photograph of a student
  • Self Declaration Certificate of the student
  • College Id Card of the Student
  • The Marksheet of the last exam passed

How to apply

Step 1: To apply for the scholarship students, students have to go to the [official website]() of the state scholarship portal.

Step 2: Select the registration option that is available at the top of the page.

Step 3: Read the details, tick the checkbox, and choose I Agree on the option.

Step 4: Further read, tick the checkbox, and select the proceed button.

Step 5: Enter the Aadhaar number and select Verify.

Step 6: Enter the OTP and mention further details.

Step 7: Select the register option and log in with the portal.

Step 8: Go to the apply button and select the scholarship scheme.

Step 9: Fill out the application form open on the screen.

Step 10: Mention all the significant details and upload the documents.

Step 11: Review the application and submit it.

Step 12: Take a printout at last for further use.
