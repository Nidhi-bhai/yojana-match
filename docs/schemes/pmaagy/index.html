Pradhan Mantri Adi Adarsh Gram Yojana (PMAAGY) eligibility, benefits, how to apply

प्रधानमंत्री आदि आदर्श ग्राम योजना (पीएमएएजीवाई) ప్రధాన మంత్రి ఆది ఆదర్శ్ గ్రామ యోజన (పీఎంఏఏజీవై)

The scheme aims to develop selected villages with significant Scheduled Tribe population into model villages by providing basic infrastructure and improving socio-economic conditions.

Benefits

Benefit amount not stated on the official page

- Financial Assistance: ₹20,38,000/- per village for infrastructure gap filling and development activities. - Infrastructure Development: Support for roads, drinking water, sanitation, education, health, and livelihood infrastructure. - Village Development Plan (VDP): Preparation and execution of need-based development plans for selected villages. - Convergence Support: Integration with existing Central and State schemes to maximize development impact. - Awards And Incentives: ₹5,00,000/- per village at State level for best performing villages and ₹10,00,000/- per village at National level for top selected villages.

Eligibility

> General Eligibility

- The village must have at least 50% Scheduled Tribe (ST) population.

- The village must have a population of at least 500 in plain areas; 250 in hilly/tribal areas.

- The village must be selected by the State/UT Government based on criteria defined in the guidelines.

> Preference / Priority

- Preference is given to villages with higher ST population concentration.

- Priority is given to villages with significant infrastructure gaps.

Documents

  • Village Selection Proposal
  • Village Development Plan (VDP)
  • Population Data Certificate (Showing ST Population Percentage)
  • Approval From District/State Authorities
  • Utilization Certificate (For Fund Release Stages)
  • Progress Reports And Monitoring Documents

How to apply

Step 1: The State Government/UT Administration identifies eligible villages.

Step 2: A Village Development Plan (VDP) is prepared for selected villages.

Step 3: The plan is approved at the district and state level.

Step 4: Proposal is submitted to the Ministry Of Tribal Affairs.

Step 5: Funds are released after approval.

Step 6: Implementation is carried out by local authorities with monitoring mechanisms.
