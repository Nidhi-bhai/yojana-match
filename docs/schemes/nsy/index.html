Nekar Samman Yojana eligibility, benefits, how to apply

नेकर सम्मान योजना నేకర్ సమ్మాన్ యోజన

In 2020 amidst the Covid pandemic, Chief Minister of Karnataka launched a mega scheme Karnataka Nekar Samman Yojana. It was a relief scheme to provide financial support to destitute weaver of state engaged in the handloom and textile industry whose livelihood was adversely affected by the pandemic.

Benefits

Benefit amount not stated on the official page

Benefits

The assistance of Rs.2000/- is transferred to the beneficiaries through the mode of Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode

Financial assistance to registered handloom weavers under the “Nekara Samman Yojana” has been increased from Rs 2,000 to Rs 5,000 as per the budget for 2022-23.

Eligibility

Eligibility

1. The beneficiary must be a resident of Karnataka. They must have a valid resident certificate.

1. All the weavers and financially distressed workers engaged in the handloom/ power loom industry and ancillary activities such as cotton, wool, and silk in the state are eligible.

1. Prospective beneficiaries must have an active bank account.

Documents

  • List of the required documents
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Ration Card
  • Proof of Resident
  • Bank Account details
  • Income Details
  • Photograph
  • Mobile Number
  • Other requisite details

How to apply

Application Process

First Applicant needs to go to the official website of the Government of Karnataka.

Then choose Covid-related services and signup.

Fill out the Nekar Samman Yojane online registration form and submit it to get the plan benefits.
