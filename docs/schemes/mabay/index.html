Mukhyamantri Aawasiye Bhu-Adhikaar Yojana eligibility, benefits, how to apply

मुख्यमंत्री आवास भू-अधिकार योजना ముఖ్యమంత్రి ఆవాసియే భు-అధికార్ యోజన

Revenue Department of Madhya Pradesh State started this scheme to provide residential plots.

Benefits

Benefit amount not stated on the official page

- Plot will be provided to all those families who do not have their own house.

Eligibility

1. The applicant family does not have a residence to live independently.

1. The applicant family has less than 5 acres of land.

1. The applicant family holds the eligibility slip for obtaining ration from the Public Distribution System (PDS) shop.

1. No member of the applicant family is income tax payer.

1. No member of the applicant family is in government service.

1. The name of the applicant is registered in the voter list till January 01, 2021 in the village where he wants residential plot.

Documents

  • Aadhar card.
  • Address proof.
  • Proof of income.
  • Proof of age.
  • Identity card.
  • Bank account statement.
  • Mobile number.
  • Passport size photograph

How to apply

1. Applicants have to visit official website.1. Click on "Apply" under the scheme name.

1. On the new page, click on the "Avedan". 1. Select the "New Application" option.
