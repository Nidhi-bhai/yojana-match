MKSYBB: eligibility, documents, how to apply

Mukhyamantri Kanya Suraksha Yojana

मुख्यमंत्री कन्या सुरक्षा योजना ముఖ్యమంత్రి కన్యా సురక్ష యోజన

The "Mukhyamantri Kanya Suraksha Yojana (MKSY)" is a welfare initiative by the Government of Bihar to combat female foeticide, improve the sex ratio, and promote birth registration.

Benefits

₹2,000/- is invested as a fixed deposit for each eligible girl child.

- ₹2,000/- is invested as a fixed deposit for each eligible girl child.

> Note: In the event of the girl child’s death, the amount is returned to the Women & Child Development Corporation, Bihar.

Eligibility

1. The Girl must be a resident of Bihar.

1. The Girl child must born on or after November 22, 2007.

1. The family must belong to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.

> Note: A maximum of 2 girl children per family can avail of the benefit.

Documents

  • Birth certificate of the girl child.
  • BPL certificate of the family.
  • Aadhaar card of the parents.
  • Bank details.
  • Death certificate of the girl child (if applicable during the intervening period).

How to apply

Step 1: Obtain the application form from the Women & Child Development Corporation or from the local designated government offices.

Step 2: Fill out the form with accurate details and attach the required documents.

Step 3: Submit the application form to the nearest government office or relevant authority.

Step 4: The documents will be verified, and eligible beneficiaries will be informed.
