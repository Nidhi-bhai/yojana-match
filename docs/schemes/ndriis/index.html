NDRI Institute Scholarship For M.Sc. And Ph.d

एम. एससी. के लिए एन. डी. आर. आई. संस्थान छात्रवृत्ति और पीएच. डी ఎం. ఎస్. సి కోసం ఎన్. డి. ఆర్. ఐ ఇన్స్టిట్యూట్ స్కాలర్షిప్ అలాగే పీహెచ్డీ

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to eligible Indian students admitted to M.Sc. (Dairying) and Ph.D. (Dairying) programmes at NDRI through monthly scholarships during the prescribed period of study.

Eligibility

- “Institute Scholarship” is offered to all students of Indian Nationality admitted to Master’s and Doctoral programmes at NDRI to provide them with financial assistance as per the rules and conditions in this document.

- Scholarships are offered to only those who do not hold any other financial Assistance/ Scholarship. As and when a student receives or accepts financial assistance from any other source, the “Institute Scholarship” will be terminated.

- The award of a scholarship will be made on receipt of the following certificates:

1. Acceptance of the award in the prescribed proforma.

1. An undertaking to follow the rules and regulations as per the conditions governing the Scholarship in the prescribed proforma.

Documents

  • Admission Proof to Master's or Doctoral Programme at NDRI
  • Proof of Indian Nationality
  • Acceptance of Award Form (Annexure-I)
  • Undertaking for Compliance with Scholarship Rules (Annexure-II)
  • Semester Registration Records
  • Progress Report (Annexure-IV)
  • Academic Performance Records/Mark Sheets
  • Employment Certificate (For In-Service Ph.D. Students, If Applicable).

How to apply

Step 1: Obtain admission to the eligible Master's or Doctoral programme at NDRI.

Step 2: Submit the Acceptance of the Award in the prescribed format.

Step 3: Submit the Undertaking to comply with scholarship rules and regulations.

Step 4: Complete registration for all prescribed courses in each semester.

Step 5: Submit periodic Progress Reports as prescribed.

Step 6: Maintain the required academic performance for continuation and renewal of the scholarship.
