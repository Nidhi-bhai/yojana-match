CMSASPS: eligibility, documents, how to apply

Chief Minister Specially Abled Samman Pension Scheme

मुख्यमंत्री विशेष रूप से सक्षम सम्मान पेंशन योजना ముఖ్యమంత్రి ప్రత్యేక వికలాంగుల పెన్షన్ పథకం

Rajasthan Government started a scholarship for Specially Abled persons.

Benefits

Benefit amount not stated on the official page

1. For women below 55 years of age and men below 58 years of age: Rs. 750/- per month.

1. To women aged 55 years and above, men aged 58 years and above but less than 75 years of age: Rs. 1,000/- per month.

1. To the beneficiaries of 75 years and above age ( male and female): Rs. 1,250/- per month.

1. To leprosy-free beneficiaries of all ages ( male and female): Rs. 1,500/- per month.

Eligibility

1. Specially abled persons of any age with 40% and above disability.

1. Applicants are suffering from any one or more following disabilities :

1. Blindness,

1. Short-sightedness,

1. Locomotor disability,

1. Leprosy free,

1. Hearing loss,

1. Mental retardation,

1. Mental illness,

1. Dwarfism (less than 3 feet 6 inches in height in case of an adult and holding a certificate issued by an authorized medical officer),

1. Naturally suffering from eunuchism.

1. Native of Rajasthan and living in Rajasthan.

1. Own combined annual income (from all sources) is up to Rs. 60,000/-.

Documents

  • Aadhar Card Copy
  • Bhamashah Card Copy

How to apply

1. Applicants have to visit the official portal. 1. Click on login.

1. If applicants are existing SSO users, log in with their SSO user and password. and If applicants don’t have SSO Id then create one by clicking “Registration”. Applicants can create SSO IDs by using Janaadhar & Google

1. Completing the SSO profile process.

1. Select Appropriate Scheme and click on the “Next” button

1. Fill out all required academic information and click on the “Submit” button
