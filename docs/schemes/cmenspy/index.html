CMENSPY: eligibility, documents, how to apply

Chief Minister Ekal Nari Samman Pension Yojana

मुख्यमंत्री एकल नारी सम्मान पेंशन योजना ముఖ్యమంత్రి ఏకల్ నారి సమ్మాన్ పెన్షన్ యోజన

The Rajasthan State government started the "Chief Minister Ekal Nari Samman Pension Yojana" scheme for women's support and empowerment.

Benefits

Benefit amount not stated on the official page

1. From 18 to 55 years old: Rs. 500/- per month.

1. From 55 to 60 years: Rs. 750/- per month.

1. From 60 to 75 years: 1000/- per month.

1. Above the age of 75 years: 1500/- per month.

Eligibility

1. The applicant's age should be 18 years or more.

1. A native of the Rajasthan state.

1. A widow/abandoned/divorced woman who does not have any source of regular income of her own for subsistence. (or)

1. Annual income from all sources should be less than Rs. 48,000/-.

Documents

  • Aadhaar Card.
  • Birth certificate.
  • Ration Card.
  • Original residence certificate.
  • Bank Account Details.
  • Applicant - Passport-size photo.
  • Income Declaration Form.
  • Death certificate of husband for widow pension.
  • Certificate issued by the court for divorcee woman pension.
  • Certificate issued by the Sub-Divisional Officer / Development Officer for abandoned women pension.

How to apply

1. The applicants have to visit on the official portal.1. On the home page, click on apply.

1. On the new page, the application form will open.

1. Now applicants need to enter the information asked in the application form.

1. After entering all the information, upload the required documents.

1. Click on the Submit.
