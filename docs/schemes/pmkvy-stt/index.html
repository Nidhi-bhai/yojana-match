PMKVY -STT: eligibility and documents

Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana 4.0 - Short-Term Training

प्रधानमंत्री कौशल विकास योजना 4.0-अल्पकालिक प्रशिक्षण ప్రధాన మంత్రి కౌశల్ వికాస్ యోజన 4.0-స్వల్పకాలిక శిక్షణ

The scheme aims to provide demand-driven, employability-focused skill training. Through this scheme, free NSQF-aligned training, On-the-Job Training, certification, and allowances are provided to Indian youth.

Benefits

Benefit amount not stated on the official page

1. The scheme provides free NSQF-aligned skill training ranging between 300 and 600 hours; courses of higher duration based on industry/sectoral demand can also be offered, and training in higher-level NSQF job roles (level 5, 6 and above) is also imparted.

1. On-the-Job Training (OJT) is provided as an inherent component to give candidates practical exposure and a live-work environment, with the duration guided by the approved Qualification Pack.

1. An induction kit consisting of a T-shirt (male) or Jacket (female) and a bag is provided; the uniform, induction kit, and participant handbook are covered up to a maximum of ₹500 per candidate.

1. Assessment and Certification is provided, with a pay-out of ₹600 per candidate per assessment given to the Sector Skill Councils (SSCs) / Awarding Bodies for overseeing and facilitating final assessments.

1. Every candidate is offered one chance of re-assessment free of cost, with the fees borne from the scheme.

1. Accidental Insurance with one year of coverage is provided to every certified candidate, as per prevalent rates (₹14.16).

1. Boarding and Lodging along with transport facilities are provided to special groups (women and Persons with Disability) and Special Areas as defined in Common Norms, and may also be extended to Aspirational, Border, Tribal-dominated, and LWE-affected districts.

1. Conveyance/Transportation cost is permissible for women and Persons with Disability (PwD) in case of non-residential training, as per Common Norms.

1. Additional support of ₹5,000 towards assistive devices, aids, and appliances is provided to all PwD candidates.

1. A free printed certificate is provided, along with a QR-code-based digital certificate issued through DigiLocker, an email/phone download link, and the Skill India Digital profile; a convocation ceremony is also held for passed-out candidates.

1. Post-certification, candidates are tracked for one year, linked with Rojgar Melas and Apprenticeship Melas, and given access through Skill India Digital to jobs, apprenticeship opportunities, and other facilities including loans for entrepreneurship; a post-certification tracking allowance of up to ₹50 per certified candidate is utilised for this purpose.

1. On passing the assessment, the candidate is awarded a graded certificate: for NSQF Level 1, 2, 3 — Grade A (85% and above), Grade B (70% to less than 85%), Grade C (50% to 70%); for NSQF Level 4 and above — Grade A (85% and above), Grade B (70% to less than 85%).

1. More than 70% biometric (AEBAS) attendance is required for each candidate to appear in the assessment.

1. The indicative average per-candidate cost is ₹9,540 for a 300-hour STT course (including Skill Hubs) and ₹13,724 for a 450-hour STT course, derived considering a base training cost of ₹27.89 per hour.

1. Funds are released to Training Providers / Training Centres / Skill Hubs in three tranches: Tranche 1 — 30% on commencement of training; Tranche 2 — 30% on the batch achieving 70% AEBAS attendance once and 50% of training being over; Tranche 3 — 40% on certification (passed candidates only). The accidental insurance coverage is valid for one year, and post-certification tracking is conducted for one year from the date of certification.

Eligibility

1. The applicant should be an Indian national.

1. The applicant should possess a valid Aadhaar.

1. The applicant should be in the age group of 15 to 45 years.

1. The applicant should fulfil the eligibility criteria of the respective job role, as defined in the course curriculum of the job role to be offered.

1. The applicant may be a candidate looking for fresh skilling, re-skilling, or upskilling.

1. The applicant may be an out-of-education candidate, a school/college dropout, or an unemployed youth.

1. The applicant should not be enrolled again in the same job role in which he/she was already trained earlier.

1. The applicant, if already certified once under PMKVY, opting for a lower NSQF-level course in the same sector, shall be discouraged; however, a higher job role in the same sector is permitted.

1. The applicant may enrol not more than twice for training in the same sector, with the second time being for a higher NSQF-aligned job role only, and with a six-month gap between the certification date of the first course and the batch start date of the subsequent course.

Documents

  • Aadhaar Card (for eKYC and mobile-based One-Time Password (OTP) profile verification).

How to apply

Step 1: The applicant first registers on the Skill India Digital (SID) platform or the mobile application developed for the purpose; profile verification is completed through mobile-based OTP (eKYC), and a de-duplication check is carried out against the SID database to prevent re-enrolment in the same job role.

Step 2: During registration, candidate profiling is done, with existing details fetched from other portals such as e-Shram and the National Career Services (NCS).

Step 3: Online counselling is offered through SID to help the candidate select an appropriate course and Training Centre; the candidate may select the nearest Training Centre based on the availability of the preferred job role and the quality of training infrastructure (rating, centre profile).

Step 4: On enrolment, the candidate is informed of the training programme details and facilities on the profile page and through a downloadable link sent to the registered number (or at the centre).

Step 5: The applicant then completes the training lifecycle — classroom/blended/online training as per the approved Qualification Pack, applicable On-the-Job Training (OJT), and assessment by the NCVET-approved Awarding Bodies and Assessment Agencies.

Step 6: On passing the assessment, the QR-code-based digital certificate is issued through DigiLocker, an email/phone download link, and the SID profile page, and a free printed certificate is provided by the Training Provider.
