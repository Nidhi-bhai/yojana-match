PM-KISAN: eligibility, documents, how to apply

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

प्रधानमंत्री किसान सम्मान निधि (पीएम-किसान) ప్రధాన మంత్రి కిసాన్ సమ్మాన్ నిధి (పీఎం-కిసాన్)

With a view to provide income support to all landholding farmers’ families in the country, having cultivable land, the Central Government has implemented a Central Sector Scheme, namely, “Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)”.

Benefits

Financial Assistance: An amount of ₹6,000/- per year in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000/- every four months is provided to each eligible family.

- Financial Assistance: An amount of ₹6,000/- per year in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000/- every four months is provided to each eligible family.

Eligibility

- All landholding farmers' families, which have cultivable land holding in their names are eligible to get benefit under the scheme.

Documents

  • Aadhaar Card.
  • Landholding papers.
  • Savings Bank Account.

How to apply

Step 1: Visit the PM-KISAN Portal.

Step 2: Click on "New Farmer Registration" option.

Step 3: Enter Your Aadhaar Number and Mobile Number.

Step 4: Enter the received OTP to authenticate your Aadhaar with UIDAI.

Step 5: Fill in the required information and upload the mandatory documents.

Step 6: Click on "Submit".
