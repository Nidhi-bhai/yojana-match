Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana eligibility, benefits, how to apply

मुख्यमंत्री कन्या विवाह योजना ముఖ్యమంత్రి కన్యా వివాహ యోజన

The purpose of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to poor families at the time of marriage, to encourage girls' education, and prevent child marriage.

Benefits

Under the scheme, an amount of ₹5,000/- will be given at the time of marriage of the girl child.

- Under the scheme, an amount of ₹5,000/- will be given at the time of marriage of the girl child.

Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a girl who is 18 years of age or older at the time of application.

1. The applicant should be a native of Bihar.

1. The applicant's family should be Below Poverty Line (BPL) card holders.

1. The annual income of the applicant's family should not exceed ₹60,000/-.

1. The applicant should be marrying a boy who is 21 years of age or older.

Documents

  • Aadhaar card
  • Basic address proof
  • Caste certificate
  • BPL ration card
  • Date of birth of the girl
  • Income certificate
  • Bank account number
  • Mobile number
  • Passport-size photo
  • Birth certificate or age certificate of the boy and the girl
  • Self-attested declaration of non-payment of dowry

How to apply

Step 1: Visit the official website: https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in/

Step 2: Go to the "Citizen Section" and click on "Register Yourself", or directly visit the registration page here:

Direct Registration Link

Step 3: Carefully fill out the application form by entering all the required personal details, address, and bank information.

Step 4: Upload all the necessary documents as specified in the application guidelines.

Step 5: Submit the completed application form through the online portal.
