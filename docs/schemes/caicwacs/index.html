State/ UT · Kerala

CA/ICWA CMA /CS Scholarship: eligibility

सीए/आईसीडब्ल्यूए सीएमए/सीएस छात्रवृत्ति సిఎ/ఐసిడబ్ల్యుఎ సిఎంఎ/సిఎస్ స్కాలర్షిప్

The scheme aims to provide scholarships to students from minority religious communities studying Chartered Accounts/Cost & Management Accounts/Company Secretaryship courses. Through this scheme, a scholarship is provided to eligible students.

Benefits

The scholarship amount of ₹15,000/- is provided.

1. The scholarship amount of ₹15,000/- is provided.

1. The scholarship is provided to students from minority religious communities.

Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Kerala.

1. The applicant must belong to Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Parsi, or Jain religious communities.

1. The applicant's annual family income should be below ₹8,00,000/-.

1. The applicant must be appearing for intermediate or final year examinations of Chartered Accounts, Cost & Management Accounts, or Company Secretaryship courses.

1. The applicant must have secured 60% marks in Plus Two/B.Com or other degrees.

1. The applicant should have an account in any nationalised bank in their own name.

> Preference/Priority/Reservation/Relaxation:

- Preference will be given to applicants belonging to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.

- In the absence of BPL applicants, applicants from minority religious communities with an annual income up to ₹8,00,000/- in the Above Poverty Line (APL) category will be considered.

- Selection will be based on merit and income.

Documents

  • Applicant's Registration Printout
  • Copy of Mark List of SSLC
  • Copy of Mark List of Plus Two/VHSE
  • Copy of Mark List of Degree (If applicable)
  • Foundation Pass Certificate (If an Intermediate student)
  • Intermediate Admission Document (If an Intermediate student)
  • Intermediate Pass Certificate (If a Final student)
  • Final Admission Document (If a Final student)
  • Proof of Study for the Course (Admission Card, Tuition Fee Receipt, ID Card, etc.)
  • Copy of the First Page of Applicant's Bank Passbook (Including Name, Account Number, Branch Code, and Branch Address)
  • Copy of Aadhaar Card OR Copy of NPR Card
  • Copy of Nativity Certificate
  • Copy of Community Certificate OR Copy of Minority Certificate
  • Original Income Certificate (from Village Office)
  • Copy of Ration Card (To prove BPL status)

How to apply

Step 1: Visit the official website of the Directorate of Minority Welfare.

Step 2: Locate and click on the "Scholarship".

Step 3: Navigate to the "Scholarship" menu link and click on "CA/ICWA (CMA)/CS Scholarship (IWCS)".

Step 4: Click on "Apply Online".

Step 5: For new scholarship applicants, first complete one-time registration and log in with your candidate details.

Step 6: In the Registration Form, carefully enter the details step-by-step under Examination details (register number/roll number), Personal details, and Scholarship details tabs.

Step 7: In the Upload details tab, upload the required documents (Photo, Signature, SSLC Certificate, Income Certificate, Ration Card Copy, Allotment Memo) in JPEG format under 100 KB size each.

Step 8: Only after uploading all required documents, the registration process will be considered complete.

Step 9: After completing the application submission, use the View/Print Application link to take a printout of the application form.

> Submission of Application Printout

- After submitting the scholarship application (either through new registration or login), click on "View/Print Application" to get a printout of your registration form.

- Submit the printout of the registration form along with all the required supporting documents to the head of the institution where you are studying, within the specified deadline.
