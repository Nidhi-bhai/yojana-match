State/ UT · Gujarat

Scholarships and Stipends: eligibility

छात्रवृत्ति और वजीफे స్కాలర్షిప్లు మరియు ఉపకార వేతనాలు

The scheme "Scholarships and Stipends" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to encourage and support national-level athletes by providing scholarships to medal winners and stipends to participants.

Benefits

Scholarships: ₹2,500/- per player is awarded to athletes who have won medals at the national level. - Stipends: ₹2,000/- per player is awarded to athletes who have participated at the national level, irrespective of winning.

- Scholarships: ₹2,500/- per player is awarded to athletes who have won medals at the national level. - Stipends: ₹2,000/- per player is awarded to athletes who have participated at the national level, irrespective of winning.

Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.

- The applicant should be a male.

- The applicant should have participated in a national-level sports event.

- The applicant should have won a medal at the national level to be eligible for the scholarship.

Documents

  • National-Level Medal Certificate (for scholarship eligibility)
  • Aadhar Card
  • Recent Passport Size Photograph
  • Residential Proof
  • Any other document as required.

How to apply

> Registration Process:

Step 1: Visit the homepage of the Sports Authority of Gujarat.

Step 2: Click on the “Registration” button on the upper-right side of the page.

Step 3: Fill in all the required details as per the form and submit it.

> Application Process:

Step 1: After registration, check your registered email for login credentials.

Step 2: Select “Player” as the user type and log in using the credentials received.

Step 3: Click on the concerned scheme name and proceed with the application.
