Mukhyamantri Rajya Bridhhabasta Pension Yojana eligibility, benefits, how to apply

मुख्यमंत्री राज्य गृहस्थ पेंशन योजना ముఖ్యమంత్రి రాజ్య వివాహ పెన్షన్ యోజన

“Mukhyamantri Rajya Bridhhabasta Pension Yojana” is a Social Security Scheme by the Department of Women, Child Development & Social Security, Government of Jharkhand. The scheme provides financial asistance to the age group of 60 years and above.

Benefits

Amount: ₹ 1,000/- per month to the eligible beneficiary.

- Amount: ₹ 1,000/- per month to the eligible beneficiary.

Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Jharkhand.

1. The applicant should have attained the age of 60 years or more.

1. The applicant should have a Voter ID card and Aadhar Card.

1. The applicant should not be a beneficiary under any other Social Security pension scheme.

1. The applicant or their spouse should not be a Retired or permanently employed in Central and State Government or in public sector undertakings.

1. The applicant or their spouse should not be a recipient of Government pension or pension from Central and State Government or from public sector undertakings.

1. Applicant’s family is not an income tax payee.

Documents

  • Copy of Voter ID card (in case, applicants age is 18 years or more).
  • Copy Aadhar Card (in case the applicant does not have an Aadhar Card they need to submit a Self-declaration for not having the same).
  • Copy of Bank Passbook.
  • Self-declaration regarding the eligibility.
  • Age Proof.

How to apply

Step 01: The interested applicant should request the prescribed format of the application from the office of Block Development Officer (BDO) / Sub Divisional Officer (SDO) / Circle Officer

Step 02: In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).

Step 03: Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the concerned authority at:

Office of the BDO in rural areas.

Office of the SDO or Circle Officer in Municipalities and notified areas.
