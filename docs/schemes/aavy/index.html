Atal Awasiya Vidyala Yojana eligibility, benefits, how to apply

अटल आवासीय विद्यालय योजना అటల్ ఆవాస్య విద్యాలయ యోజన

The Atal Awasiya Vidyalaya Yojana is an initiative launched by the Uttar Pradesh government to provide free education to children of construction workers employed in the informal sector.

Benefits

Benefit amount not stated on the official page

1. Free education, accommodation, and meals

1. Medical facilities

1. Sports and recreational activities

1. Access to a nurturing environment that fosters personal growth

Eligibility

1. Children destitute during the Corona period, identified by the Women Welfare Department, Lucknow.

1. Children for the Chief Minister Child Service Scheme (General) are eligible.

1. Orphan children and offspring of duly registered construction workers, who have completed at least 01 year (365 days) membership period of the board after registration.

1. A maximum of 02 children per registered worker can avail educational benefits.

Documents

  • Parents' Labor Registration cards.
  • Child birth certificate
  • Mark sheet of previous class attended.
  • Medical certificate (if required).
  • Passport size photograph.

How to apply

> How to get register and apply for the scheme

1. Applicant must visit the below offices

- Nearest labor office.

- Tehsildar of the concerned tehsil.

- Block Development Officer of the concerned development block.

1. Get the application form and fill out the completely.

1. Attach required documents in the prescribed format.

1. Submit the application form in the same office.
