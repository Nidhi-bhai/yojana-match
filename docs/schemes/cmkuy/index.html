State · Bihar

CMKUY: eligibility, documents, how to apply

Chief Minister Kanya Utthan Yojana

मुख्यमंत्री कन्या उत्थान योजना ముఖ్యమంత్రి కన్యా ఉత్థాన్ యోజన

A Scheme Chief Minister Kanya Utthan Yojana started under the Social Welfare Department by Bihar State Government for the social empowerment of females.

Benefits

Benefit amount not stated on the official page

Target Group

1. To the girl child born from the date of issue of notification.

1. Given only to two girl children of the family.

1. Payable to a family resident in Bihar.

Nature of Grant:

1. Rs. 2,000/- will be transferred to the Parents/guardians of every girl child in the age group from 0 to 1 year.

1. After the completion of 1 year of the girl child and Aadhaar registration, the amount of Rs. 1,000/- will be payable again in the same account.

Eligibility

1. The girl children should be residents of Bihar.

1. The birth has been duly registered.

1. The benefit of this scheme will be payable to the girl child born from the date of issue of the notification.

1. This benefit will be given only to two girl children of a family.

1. On the first day of the date of the beneficiary application of the scheme, Rs. 2,000 and for the second child of 1-2 years, Rs. 1,000 will be payable to the mother/father/guardian of the girl child after fulfilling all the prescribed conditions.

Documents

  • Photocopy of the Residential Certificate.
  • Photocopy of Birth Registration Certificate.
  • Photocopy of Identity Card.
  • In respect of two girl children, the columns of the prescribed application form will be attested by the Anganwadi worker.

How to apply

1. To take advantage of this scheme, the application form will be available at all Anganwadi centers of the Child Development Project.

1. The application form for this scheme will be made available free of cost by the maids of all Anganwadi centers.

1. For the benefit of this scheme, the applicant will fill the application in the prescribed form and submit it at the concerned Anganwadi centers along with all the necessary documents.

1. Child Development Project Officer will be the acceptance officer for this.

1. Registering the application form filled by the acceptance officer, the Assistant Director will make it available to the District Child Protection Unit.

1. Office of the Assistant Director District Child Protection Unit will compile the list of applications and take action to transfer the allocated amount to the beneficiary's mother/father/guardian's account.
