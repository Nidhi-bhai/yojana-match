State/ UT · Gujarat

Chief Minister Scholarship Scheme: eligibility

मुख्यमंत्री छात्रवृत्ति योजना ముఖ్యమంత్రి స్కాలర్షిప్ పథకం

The scheme "Chief Minister Scholarship Scheme" by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious students for pursuing medical, engineering, diploma, and higher education courses.

Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.

- The applicant must be a student.

- The applicant must have passed the Class 10th or Class 12th examination.

- The applicant must have secured admission in a diploma course, a degree course after diploma, or a degree course after Class 12th.

- The applicant must be pursuing medical, dental, engineering, technology, pharmacy, Ayurveda, homeopathy, nursing, physiotherapy, para-medical, or higher education courses.

- The applicant must have a family annual income of less than or equal to ₹4,50,000/-.

Documents

  • Class 10th/12th Marksheet.
  • Diploma/Degree Admission Proof (as applicable).
  • Income Certificate (showing annual family income ≤ ₹4,50,000).
  • Proof of Residence (Aadhaar Card/Voter ID/any valid Government ID).
  • Bank Account Details (for DBT).
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable and required for merit consideration).
  • Recent Passport Size Photograph.
  • Any other details as required.

How to apply

Step 1: Students must register on the Official Website using their Standard 10th or Standard 12th seat number. Do not use any spaces or special characters when entering the seat number.

Step 2: Students applying for a Diploma to Degree course should use their Diploma enrollment number for registration.

Step 3: The student's name must be entered exactly as it appears on their Standard 10 or 12 original marksheet.

Step 4: Enter the passing year according to the marksheet (e.g., 2024).

Provide a personal mobile number that will remain active throughout the entire course of study, as important updates will be sent via SMS to this number.

Step 5: Enter and verify your 12-digit Aadhaar card number.

Step 6: Fill in the correct ration card details, including the ration card number and the student's member ID number.

Step 7: Provide accurate bank account details and an active email ID.

Step 8: Enter the percentage from the marksheet; if percentages are not available, calculate them according to the rules of the university or board.

Step 9: When selecting a stream, students from Arts and Commerce streams should choose "General Stream," while Science students should select "Science Stream".
