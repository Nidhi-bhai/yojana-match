How YojanaMatch works

YojanaMatch is an independent website built by Nidhi Karuturi. It is not a Government of India website and it is not a state government website.

The database is a structured subset of schemes published on myScheme, the official scheme portal maintained for the Government of India. Each record keeps the official name, eligibility text, benefits text, documents, an apply link when myScheme lists one, the source URL, and the date the record was checked.

If the official page does not state a single benefit amount, that field is left blank. Amounts are not estimated.

The catalogue has 239 schemes copied from myScheme on 2026-10-27. It is a subset, not every scheme in India. “Copied from myScheme on” is the copy date, not a claim that every link was checked by hand.

A scheme is confirmed only when every eligibility condition in the official text maps to a question the form asks: state, age, gender, category, occupation, disability, income band, and the optional questions for ex-servicemen, freedom fighters, orphans, widows, police wards, and ration card / SECC / below-poverty-line status. Scholarships are treated as student-only. A close date before 2026-10-27 removes the scheme from matches and from the PDF. Its page shows a closed badge.

Where the official text states a further condition this form does not ask, the scheme is listed under “Possibly eligible, check conditions”. That section is not a match and it is not put in the PDF.
